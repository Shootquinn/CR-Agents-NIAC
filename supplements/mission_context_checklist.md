## Mission paragraph checklist

The Writer and the Fact-Checker answer yes or no to each line. Any "no" means rewrite the paragraph.

1. Is a single representative mission named specifically? (R-c08)
2. Is the mission architecture stated? (R-c08)
3. Are the science objectives stated? (R-c08)
4. Are the mission operations stated? (R-c08)
5. Is the system-level design stated? (R-c08)
6. Is applicability to other missions stated? (R-c08)
7. Are the mission-context assumptions stated in one "Assumptions:" sentence, with no inline "(assumed)" tags? (R-c12)
8. Is a size, weight and power estimate present? (R-c09, beneficial, not mandatory)
9. Does a back-of-the-envelope calculation support the impact in the exemplar mission? (R-c06)
10. Is a comparison with alternatives and the state of the art present? (R-c10)
11. Does mission context appear in the first two paragraphs of the body? (ACTION, CLOVER and TitanAir all open with context)
12. Mission quadrant leads with the new capability. Does the overview chart do this? (R-c03)
13. Does one sentence name the wholly new capability that no existing or previously explored mission has?
14. Does the phrase "wholly new" appear 1 to 3 times?
15. Is the text free of Phase II content, budget and team? (R-c14, R-c19)
