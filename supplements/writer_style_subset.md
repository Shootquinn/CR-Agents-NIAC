## Writer style subset
1. Lead each paragraph with its claim in the first sentence, then support it.
2. Give each paragraph one idea; start a new paragraph when the idea changes.
3. Vary three consecutive sentences so their lengths differ by more than 3 words.
4. Name the actor: write "the team measures", not "measurements are taken".
5. Put a concrete number before any adjective, and cut the adjective if the number suffices.
6. Define each abbreviation once on first use, then use only the abbreviation.
7. End each section on its result, not on a caveat.
8. State the problem in the first two sentences of the proposal.
9. Write "to learn X, we do Y" for each task so the question precedes the method.
10. Present the data before the claim drawn from it.
11. Put the new information at the end of each sentence and the known topic at the start.
12. Keep the verb within five words of its subject.
13. Replace each nominalization with its verb ("investigate", not "conduct an investigation").
14. Use plain verbs that show a mechanism; replace "facilitate", "mediate", and "evaluate" with the action.
15. Delete intensifiers such as very, extremely, really, and clearly.
16. Use the passive voice only when the actor is unknown or the acted-on is the topic.
17. Begin a sentence with the previous sentence's final idea when linking two sentences.
18. Place a limitation next to the claim it limits, in a subordinate clause, and finish the sentence on the claim.
19. Write numbers with units and one comparison baseline each time.
20. Use the same term for the same thing throughout; never swap synonyms.
21. Use a table or figure only with one sentence that cites it and states its single finding.
22. Cut any sentence that repeats a number or finding already stated.
23. Cut sentences that praise the work's rigor, honesty, or importance without a fact.
24. Avoid the 8 patterns in editor_grep_list.md, and use commas, periods, or colons in place of dashes.
25. End the proposal on what the work establishes and the concrete next question it opens.
