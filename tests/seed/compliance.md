## Compliance tests (cycle label in each row)
Source governs: the current NASA solicitation. Regenerate this file at intake when the cycle changes.

| ID | name | criterion (PASS when) | priority | source | cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Relevance to NASA mission | A sentence names a US government or commercial benefit in space or aeronautics (a named agency, mission class or market) that the concept delivers. FAIL when no such sentence exists. | CRITICAL | SOL p.19 | 2027 A1 |
| C02 | Technical credibility | PASS unless one of two faults is found. Fault 1: a claim of energy, power or mass output above input with no source and no calculation. Fault 2: two stated numbers that contradict each other (quote both). A claim that is only bold or unsourced is not a fault here; C08 and the Fact-Checker cover it. | CRITICAL | SOL p.19 | 2027 A1 |
| C03 | Programmatic credibility | The draft names at least one implementation barrier (budget, policy, supply or infrastructure) and one approach to overcome it. | CRITICAL | SOL p.19-20 | 2027 A1 |
| C04 | Innovation present | The draft states in one sentence what is new, names one prior effort or alternative, and states one difference from it. | MAJOR | SOL p.20 | 2027 A1 |
| C05 | Impact present | A sentence states the benefit as a number with units against a named alternative, and the number traces to the C09 calculation. | MAJOR | SOL p.20 | 2027 A1 |
| C06 | Mission present | One sentence names the representative mission with its destination and its host platform (vehicle, lander, habitat or station), and the draft describes at least three of: delivery method, operations, duration, science objective, system design. The quote must hold the destination name and the word mission. FAIL when no sentence names both. | MAJOR | SOL p.20 | 2027 A1 |
| C07 | Technical Approach present | The draft names at least two technical unknowns and states how Phase I will study each one. | MAJOR | SOL p.21 | 2027 A1 |
| C08 | Feasibility calculation | The body shows a back-of-the-envelope calculation with an input number with units, one operation and a result with units. FAIL when no such calculation is in body text. | MAJOR | SOL p.7 | 2027 A1 |
| C09 | Impact calculation | The body shows a second back-of-the-envelope calculation of the benefit in the named mission, with a result compared to an alternative. | MAJOR | SOL p.7 | 2027 A1 |
| C10 | One named mission | Exactly one representative mission is named and given an architecture. Other missions appear only under a label such as extension. | MAJOR | SOL p.9 | 2027 A1 |
| C11 | SWaP estimate | The body states mass (kg or g), power (W) and size or volume (m or m3) for the mission system. | MINOR | FAQ Q57 | 2027 A1 |
| C12 | Implementation path | The draft describes at least one path from the concept to a flown system in two or more ordered steps. | MAJOR | SOL p.8 | 2027 A1 |
| C13 | Entry TRL | The body contains the string TRL followed by 1 or 2 as the entry level. FAIL when TRL is absent. | MAJOR | SOL p.8 | 2027 A1 |
| C14 | Phase I objectives | The body lists at least three top-level Phase I objectives, each on its own line or numbered. | MAJOR | SOL p.11 | 2027 A1 |
| C15 | Comparison with alternatives | The body names at least two alternatives or the state of the art and states one difference from each. | MAJOR | SOL p.9 | 2027 A1 |
| C16 | No Phase II content | The draft contains no discussion of a later phase and no request for resources for later proposals. | MAJOR | SOL p.7-8 | 2027 A1 |
| C17 | No other proposals | No sentence refers to a different proposal. | MAJOR | FAQ Q41 | 2027 A1 |
| C18 | Self-sufficient | The draft contains no URL (http or www) and no sentence that sends the reader to an outside document to follow a claim. | MAJOR | NRA p.13 | 2027 A1 |
| C19 | Mission assumptions | A sentence or list labeled assumptions names at least two mission-context assumptions. | MAJOR | SOL p.7 | 2027 A1 |
| C20 | AI use cited | The references hold an entry for each AI tool used giving program name, version number, date and how it was used. FAIL when any of the four is missing. | CRITICAL | NRA p.11-12 | 2027 A1 |
| C21 | Not previously explored | No sentence admits the concept was studied before without naming the new aspect. | ADVISORY MINOR | blog Prompt 1 | 2027 A1 |
| C22 | Not incremental | One sentence names an aspect that is not an expected future capability. | ADVISORY MINOR | blog Prompt 1 | 2027 A1 |
| C23 | Not too narrow | The concept sits in a full mission system (not a standalone instrument) and one other use is labeled as an extension. | ADVISORY MINOR | blog Prompt 1 | 2027 A1 |
| W1 | Wholly new capability | The Mission text states in one sentence the wholly new capability that no existing or previously explored mission has. Quote the sentence. | CRITICAL | SOL p.22 | 2027 A1 |
| W2 | Leap quantified | If the concept improves existing work, the Impact or Mission text gives the leap as a factor (a number such as 10 times), not an adjective. PASS when the concept is wholly new. | CRITICAL | SOL p.22 | 2027 A1 |
