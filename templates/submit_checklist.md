# Submit Checklist (answer yes or no)

1. Does the proposal show relevance to NASA's mission, with national and commercial benefit to space or aeronautics? (SOL p.19)
2. Does it acknowledge and defend any conflict with established physics, so it is technically credible? (SOL p.19)
3. Does it name the barriers to implementation and give a plausible path, so it is programmatically credible? (SOL p.19-20)
4. Is the overview chart exactly 1 page in landscape? (SOL p.10)
5. Is the Technical and Management Section 3 pages or fewer? (SOL p.10)
6. Do references, if any, start on page 5? (SOL p.10-11; FAQ Q2)
7. Are margins at least 1 inch on US Letter paper? (SOL p.9)
8. Does body text and caption density stay at or under 15 characters per horizontal inch? (SOL p.9)
9. Does body text stay at or under 5.5 lines per vertical inch? (SOL p.9)
10. Is every AI use cited in the references with program, version, date and time, and how it was used? (FAQ Q45)
11. Is the PI name and institution absent from the overview chart? (SOL p.10)
12. Is everything combined in a single PDF file? (FAQ Q20)
13. Does the Mission section state, in one sentence, the wholly new capability no existing or explored mission has? (SOL p.22-23)
14. Is overview chart text Arial 12 point or larger? (SOL p.10)
15. Are budget and proposed team absent from the body and cover page? (SOL p.10; FAQ Q22)
