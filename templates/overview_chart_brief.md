# Overview chart brief

Fill every field. Leave none blank. Source every quantity from the intake summary, the outline or the sources digest.

1. Short title: 4 to 9 words on one line. Acronym plus expansion is common. No PI or institution name.
   Optional: letters to underline for the acronym.
2. Hero graphic: file name in figures/; what it shows in one sentence; render, concept art or photo. About 4:3, wide. No text labels in the image. Key content sits in the center.
3. Innovation (top left): 3 to 5 bullets, 45 to 60 words. What is new, and how it compares with the state of the art and the alternatives. Include 1 or 2 quantified claims.
4. Impact (top right): 3 to 5 bullets, 40 to 55 words. The difference if it succeeds, including one Earth or spin-off use.
5. Mission (bottom left): 3 to 5 bullets, 45 to 60 words. The named representative mission (place, scale, platform). First line contains "wholly new" or "no mission has".
6. Approach (bottom right): 4 to 6 bullets, 45 to 60 words. Each bullet starts with a task verb. No schedule, team or cost.
7. Format check: 11 x 8.5 in wide page; the four labels Innovation, Impact, Mission, Approach in template positions; one bullet level; text at least Arial 12 pt; no empty quadrant; total 200 to 240 words (the boxes hold about 60 words each at Arial 12 pt; the example charts ran 200 to 360).
